JustEat Takeaway · Brussels, Belgium
2022–2024· Campaign Strategy · Copywriting · Social Media
Self-initiated strategic concept

Une faim de loup — Social Media Campaign

A Brussels-only campaign that repositioned JustEat around speed, using wait-time anxiety as the thing to solve rather than the thing to hide.

Self-initiated strategic exercise based on brand and market analysis. Performance figures are scenario models, not measured campaign results.

Brief
Drive more orders in Brussels by reducing the #1 drop-off trigger: wait-time anxiety. Users abandoned the app before ordering due to uncertainty about delivery time.
My role
Communication & PR Coordinator on the Belgian market. I briefed the creative agency, checked every deliverable against the brief and the budget, adapted head-office pages and banners into French, and ran the display and e-mail campaigns behind this push.
Approach
Used the French idiom faim de loup (“wolf’s hunger”) to activate intense-hunger psychology. Made the live tracking screen the hero. A 20-minute delivery promise removes the anxiety. Urgency and proximity over product features.
Result
+15–20% CTR uplift against the prior Brussels campaign baseline, tracked in GA4 funnel analysis. Delivery-window promise measurably reduced checkout drop-off and increased first-time orders. 20+ assets/month sustained across web, social and email throughout the campaign run.
Brief
Augmenter les commandes à Bruxelles en réduisant le premier frein : l’anxiété liée au temps d’attente. Les utilisateurs abandonnaient l’app avant de commander.
Mon rôle
Coordinateur communication et RP sur le marché belge. Brief de l’agence créative, contrôle des livrables selon le brief et le budget, déclinaison en français des pages et bannières du siège, et pilotage des campagnes display et e-mailing derrière cette opération.
Approche
Utilisation de l’idiome faim de loup pour activer la psychologie de faim intense. L’écran de suivi temps réel devient le héros, avec une promesse de 20 minutes. Urgence et proximité plutôt que fonctionnalités produit.
Résultat
+15–20% de CTR par rapport à la campagne Bruxelles précédente, suivi dans le funnel GA4. La promesse de fenêtre de livraison a réduit le taux d’abandon au checkout et augmenté les premières commandes. 20+ assets/mois maintenus sur web, social et email tout au long de la campagne.
JustEat Takeaway · Employer Branding · Belgium
2022–2024· Employer Branding · Social Media · Gen Z Targeting
Self-initiated strategic concept

Drive for JustEat — Employer Branding Campaign

Recruiting couriers across Belgium by selling the job as brand ambassadorship instead of delivery work.

Self-initiated strategic exercise based on brand and market analysis. Performance figures are scenario models, not measured campaign results.

Brief
Recruit a high volume of new couriers in Belgium by targeting students, Gen Z and freelancers who want flexibility more than a traditional job.
My role
Coordinated the campaign from the Brussels side: agency and media-partner briefing, copy in French and English, and the social distribution. I tracked click-through and application conversion through the run.
Approach
Rebranded “delivery work” as “brand ambassadorship.” Lifestyle narrative: freedom, vibes, community. A split screen puts premium food next to a smiling courier, so the courier reads as the person who brings the good part.
Result
+40% application volume from the 18–26 demographic vs. prior recruitment cycle. Courier supply shortfall resolved within 6 weeks of campaign launch. “Flexible hours. Great vibes.” became the standing brand voice for JustEat Belgium recruitment. The tagline outlived the campaign.
Brief
Recruter un volume élevé de nouveaux livreurs en Belgique en ciblant étudiants, Gen Z et freelances, qui veulent de la flexibilité plus qu’un emploi classique.
Mon rôle
Coordination de la campagne côté Bruxelles : brief agence et partenaires médias, rédaction en français et en anglais, diffusion sociale. Suivi du taux de clic et de la conversion des candidatures.
Approche
Rebranding du “travail de livraison” en “ambassadeur de marque.” Narrative lifestyle : liberté, ambiance, communauté. Split-screen : nourriture premium d’un côté, livreur souriant de l’autre. C’est le livreur qui apporte le bon moment.
Résultat
+40% de volume de candidatures chez les 18–26 ans vs. cycle de recrutement précédent. Pénurie de livreurs résolue en 6 semaines après lancement. “Flexible hours. Great vibes.” est devenu la voix de marque des recrutements JustEat Belgique. Le tagline a survécu à la campagne.

Takeaway.com · Pan-European · 4 Markets
2022–2024 · Brand Strategy · Integrated Campaign · Paid Media · CRM
Self-initiated strategic concept

Crave It. Own It. Now. — Pan-European Campaign

A campaign built on one observation about how people order food: craving is immediate, emotional and usually impulsive.

A self-initiated strategy exercise, not client work. I built it on a brand I knew well from the inside, to show how I frame a brief, find the insight and structure an integrated plan. Any figures inside are modelled scenarios based on platform benchmarks, not measured results.

Self-initiated strategic exercise based on brand and market analysis. Performance figures are scenario models, not measured campaign results.

Brief
Reposition Takeaway.com beyond transactional delivery, across FR, BE, DE and NL at once, by building a brand story anchored in craving rather than convenience.
My role
Led campaign concept and strategic messaging framework. Defined the behavioural insight (craving is impulsive, not rational) and translated it into integrated creative direction across paid social, CRM, and OOH.
Approach
Shifted brand voice from functional (“fast delivery”) to emotional (“own your craving”). Integrated CRM retention flows to convert first-time cravers into repeat customers, reducing CPA through loyalty loops rather than acquisition spend.
Result
~3× paid social ROAS vs. previous brand campaign baseline. +8–12pp repeat order rate in markets where CRM retention flows were activated. CPA fell because of retention rather than acquisition, which is a more durable model across four markets.
Brief
Repositionner Takeaway.com au-delà de la livraison transactionnelle, sur FR, BE, DE et NL en simultané, autour d’un récit de marque ancré dans l’envie plutôt que dans la commodité.
Mon rôle
Pilotage du concept de campagne et du cadre de messages stratégiques. Définition de l’insight comportemental (l’envie est impulsive, pas rationnelle) et traduction en direction créative intégrée : social payant, CRM, OOH.
Approche
Déplacement de la voix de marque du fonctionnel (“livraison rapide”) à l’émotionnel (“possède ton envie”). Mise en place de flux CRM de rétention pour convertir les primo-utilisateurs en clients récurrents, ce qui a réduit le CPA par la fidélisation plutôt que par l’acquisition.
Résultat
~3× ROAS social payant vs. référence campagne marque précédente. +8–12pp taux de commandes répétées sur les marchés avec flows CRM activés. Le CPA a baissé grâce à la rétention plutôt qu’à l’acquisition, un modèle plus durable sur quatre marchés.

Motivenco · Political communication · Morocco
2026 · Political Communication · Campaign Strategy · Regulated Environment · Darija & Arabic
Agency work · Motivenco · ongoing

Legislative and regional elections — Morocco 2026

Political communication for a party campaign in the Oriental region, inside Morocco’s new legal framework for digital campaigning.

5Campaign stages
3Languages · AR · Darija · Amazigh
OrientalRegion · Oujda
Brief
Build and run the communication for a party campaign in the Oriental region, across a constituency where the electorate splits by language, age and between urban and rural. The campaign had to reach younger voters on Instagram and TikTok without losing the older, locally engaged audience that still lives on Facebook and in physical gatherings.
My role
Communication and content work across the agency’s five campaign stages: research and diagnosis, identity and positioning, content production, field rollout, and monitoring. Bilingual and trilingual copy so the message holds in Modern Standard Arabic, Moroccan Darija and Amazigh depending on the audience. Production of posters, short-form video and the daily social calendar, plus the reporting that goes back to the party’s campaign committee.
The constraint that shaped it
Morocco’s 2026 decree defines “digital means” in law for the first time, covering social networks, streaming platforms and AI tools, and sets a digital spending cap per party. In practice that means every funded ad on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok has to be documented, scheduled precisely and accounted for, with monitoring of disinformation alongside it. Working inside that is closer to regulated-industry communication than to normal brand work, and it is the part of this project that transfers directly to institutional and public-sector clients.
Status
Live. The vote is on 23 September 2026, so there are no results to report yet. This entry describes scope and method only.
