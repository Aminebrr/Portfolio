01Client work · in-house

Une faim
de loup

Just Eat Takeaway.com · Brussels · 2022–2024

A Brussels campaign about the wait. People were leaving the app without ordering because they didn’t know when the food would arrive, so we put the live tracking screen at the centre and promised 20 minutes.

CampaignCopywritingDisplay & e-mail

Brief

Get more orders in Brussels. The main reason people dropped off before ordering was not knowing how long delivery would take.

My role

Communication & PR Coordinator for Belgium. I briefed the creative agency, checked the work against the brief and budget, adapted head-office pages and banners into French, and ran the display and e-mail campaigns.

The idea

“Avoir une faim de loup” means being starving. Paired with the tracking screen and the 20-minute promise, the ad answers the one question people have before they order.

Result

+15–20% click-through on the display and e-mail campaigns compared with the previous Brussels campaigns. 20+ assets a month across web, social and e-mail during the run.

04Agency work · Motivenco

Morocco
elections 2026

Motivenco · Oriental region, Oujda · 2026

Communication for a party campaign in the Oriental region, under Morocco’s new rules on digital campaigning.

Public communicationArabic · Darija · AmazighRegulated

Brief

The electorate is split by language, by age and between town and countryside. Younger voters are on Instagram and TikTok, older ones on Facebook and at local meetings. The campaign had to reach both.

My role

Content and communication across the agency’s five stages, from research to monitoring. Copy in Modern Standard Arabic, Darija and Amazigh. Posters, short videos, the daily social calendar and reporting to the campaign committee.

The constraint

A 2026 decree defines digital campaigning in law for the first time and caps digital spending per party. Every paid post had to be documented and scheduled, with disinformation monitoring alongside. It works a lot like communication in a regulated sector.

Status

The vote took place on 23 September 2026. Campaign results belong to the party and aren’t published here.
