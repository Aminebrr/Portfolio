Concept · personal project Takeaway.com · FR, BE, DE, NL

Crave it.Own it.Now.

A brand campaign idea for Takeaway.com, built on one observation: a craving is impulsive, and it often hits late in the evening.

I made this on my own, not for a client. I knew the brand from inside the group, and I wanted to show how I would take a brief from insight to plan. The starting points below are my assumptions, and every figure on this page is modelled, not measured.

BrandTakeaway.com
MarketsFR · BE · DE · NL
Length12 weeks (planned)
My partStrategy and copy

01 Starting point

What I assumed about the brand before writing anything. These are hypotheses for the exercise, not company data.

Business

Growth costs more and more in France and Germany, and repeat orders aren’t rising as fast as new customers.

Brand

People see the app as practical and good value, but they don’t feel much for it.

Objective

Make the brand feel present when hunger hits, so people think of it first and come back more often.

Audience

18 to 34 year-olds in cities, plus busy professionals. Both order late in the evening and when they’re with friends.

02 Strategy

Three ideas that the whole plan hangs on.

1

Be there at the craving

Plan around the moments people want food, not fixed mealtimes. Time of day, weather and context decide when and what we show.

2

Turn one order into a habit

Use CRM and a light loyalty mechanic so a first order leads to the next one, with reminders and small rewards.

3

Show up in culture

Work with creators and native formats so cravings become something people share, not only something they see in an ad.

03 12-week plan

Four phases, each with one job.

  1. Weeks 1–3

    Teaser

    “What are you craving?” posters and social posts in a few cities, with a QR code to a short quiz. Creators get the idea early to start the conversation.

    OOHSocialCreatorsQuiz
  2. Weeks 4–7

    Launch

    The full reveal on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and paid social, with versions that change with the time of day and the weather. Loyalty messaging starts here too.

    Paid socialDCOLoyalty
  3. Weeks 8–10

    Community

    Creator and user content keeps it going. E-mail and push use past orders, and retargeting brings quiz players back with a clearer offer.

    UGCCRMRetargeting
  4. Weeks 11–12

    Convert

    Short offers, win-back e-mails and a personal order recap to close on loyalty.

    OffersWin-backRecap
